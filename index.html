Visit strategy

Plan it right.

One section combining the three practical things visitors usually need: when to come, how to plan a family day, and what to pack.

Best time to visit

Use the opening calendar to choose a day that matches your schedule, then check the official site again for event-specific hours.

Early opening hours are easiest to plan around.
Weekdays outside school-holiday peaks can be easier for a full coaster day.
Magic Nights and seasonal events can change the rhythm of the day.

Family planning

Start with attractions that match your group's height limits, then work outward through zones instead of crossing the park repeatedly.

Check the height requirement of major rides before queueing.
Mix bigger coasters with family and water attractions for an easier day.
Keep meal, rest and shade breaks in the plan instead of treating them as an afterthought.

What to bring

Pack for a long outdoor day with a mix of dry land, splash attractions and changing weather.

Comfortable shoes and clothes you can move in all day.
Sun protection, water bottle and a light rain layer.
A small dry bag or waterproof pouch for valuables around water attractions.
How it grew

A decade of
expansion.

The original site's timeline, rebuilt into a cleaner visual story.

2014Grand opening

Park inception

Energylandia officially opens in Zator with initial family and kiddie coasters.

2015First thrills

Adrenaline leap

Energuś, Dragon Roller Coaster and Roller Coaster Mayan join the park.

2016–17Expansion

Launches & shuttles

Formuła arrives, followed by Boomerang, Circus Coaster and Happy Loops.

2018Megapark

Hyperion & Speed

The park adds Hyperion and the towering Speed water coaster.

2019Dragon Zone

Smoczy Gród & Zadra

The medieval Dragon Zone opens with Zadra, Frida and Draken.

2021Aqualantis

Sunken city launch

Aqualantis opens with Abyssus and Light Explorers.

2024Sweet Valley

Immersive expansion

Sweet Valley debuts with Choco Chip Creek and Honey Harbour.
